# Prompt 01 — the original (Seedance 2.5, text-to-video, 30s, 16:9, 720p)

This is the exact prompt that produced the first video — the one that went around. It's also the one that gave a woman three hands at the water tap, so the shipped version is 25 seconds, not 30. Both facts are part of the story.

Where it came from: I started with a prompt written for a **trailer park in Alabama** and translated it. Not word-for-word — a trailer park has no Kenyan equivalent — but the *social* equivalent: a "plot," the rented rooms around a shared courtyard where everyone's business is everyone else's. That translation is the whole reason the clip feels real.

```
This is messy, fast-cut 1980s home video shot on a VHS camcorder in a "plot" — a cluster of rented rooms around a shared dirt courtyard — in Nairobi's Jericho estate. Cuts happen very frequently, around every 1.5 seconds. The camera jumps quickly between lots of different shots: different angles of the mabati (corrugated iron) roofs and doorways, people outside, the dirt courtyard, clotheslines strung between buildings, a parked Peugeot 504 or VW Kombi, jerry cans by the communal water tap, and random moments happening around the compound. The editing feels spontaneous and slightly chaotic, as if the person filming keeps changing their mind about what to show. The camera is extremely shaky and handheld, with constant movement and loose framing. The footage has the distinctive texture and color of real 1980s VHS video. Natural sound only, recorded with the built-in microphone. The result must feel like genuine, fast and chaotic home video from someone randomly filming life in a Nairobi housing estate in the 1980s.
```

## What it got right
- The Peugeot 504. Badge and all. I asked for "a Peugeot 504 or VW Kombi" and got a correct navy 504.
- The cut rhythm, the dust, the light, the laundry, the jerry cans at the tap.
- One moment I'd call perfect: a man bent over something, straightens up, notices the camera, holds it for a beat, looks away. That's the benchmark for everything after.

## What it got wrong
- **Three hands** on the woman at the tap. Whole shot removed (see the example README for the cut).
- **Wrong language.** The shouting sounded West African, not Swahili. I never specified — my fault, not the model's.
- Clothes on the line pinned from the wrong side. Minor, left in.

## What I'd change (and did — see prompt 02 and 03)
Two lines: name the language and region explicitly, and give the model anatomy instructions for any hand-heavy action.
